About Us

Discover the story behind Kwa Shumilensizwa.

Our Story

Kwa Shumilensizwa began with a simple idea: to bring people together through good, home-cooked food.

The story started in KwaMashu, where the owner developed a passion for preparing traditional meals inspired by the food he grew up eating and the recipes shared within his family and community.

What began as a small operation from a home kitchen gradually became a way of sharing authentic Kasi flavours with friends, neighbours and members of the wider community.

As the number of customers increased, so did the ambition behind the business. The menu expanded while remaining focused on generous portions, familiar Kasi flavours and the feeling of a home-cooked meal.

Our Mission

To provide customers with delicious, authentic and satisfying Kasi meals while delivering friendly service and a convenient food-ordering experience.

Our Food Philosophy

Authentic Flavours

We celebrate traditional South African and Kasi flavours inspired by home cooking.

Generous Portions

Our meals are prepared to be satisfying and filling.

Quality Food

We focus on providing tasty meals made with care.

Community

Food brings people together, and community is at the heart of Kwa Shumilensizwa.
